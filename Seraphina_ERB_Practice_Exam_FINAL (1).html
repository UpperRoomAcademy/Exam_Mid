Fraction Equivalence & Ordering
Finding equivalent fractions · Comparing with < > = · Ordering least to greatest
⚡ Your Focus Area
1
Which fraction is equivalent to 23?
A
3/6
B
4/9
C
8/12
D
6/8
✓ Answer: C — 8/12
1
Multiply BOTH numerator and denominator by the same number.
2
2 × 4 = 8 and 3 × 4 = 12 → 8/12. ✓
3
3/6 = 1/2 ≠ 2/3.  4/9: 3×2=6≠9.  6/8 = 3/4 ≠ 2/3.
2
Lena says 34 and 68 are equivalent. Which number sentence BEST proves this?
A
3 + 3 = 6 and 4 + 4 = 8
B
3 × 2 = 6 and 4 × 2 = 8
C
6 – 3 = 3 and 8 – 4 = 4
D
3 × 4 = 12 and 6 × 8 = 48
✓ Answer: B — 3 × 2 = 6 and 4 × 2 = 8
1
Fractions are equivalent when you multiply BOTH top and bottom by the SAME number.
2
Multiplier is 2 for both: 3×2=6 and 4×2=8. ✓
3
Adding (A) or subtracting (C) does NOT prove equivalence.
3
Which set of fractions is ordered from LEAST to GREATEST?
A
3/4,  1/2,  1/4
B
2/3,  1/3,  3/3
C
1/8,  3/4,  1/2
D
1/4,  1/2,  3/4
✓ Answer: D — 1/4, 1/2, 3/4
1
Convert to eighths: 1/4=2/8, 1/2=4/8, 3/4=6/8.
2
2/8 < 4/8 < 6/8, so 1/4 < 1/2 < 3/4. ✓
3
A is greatest→least (the opposite direction).

Unit Fractions & Operations
Decomposing fractions · Adding & subtracting with like denominators · Mixed numbers
⚡ Your Focus Area
9
Which shows 38 broken apart using unit fractions?
A
1/8 + 1/8
B
1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3
C
1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8
D
3/8 + 3/8 + 3/8
✓ Answer: C — 1/8 + 1/8 + 1/8
1
A unit fraction has 1 as the numerator (1/8, 1/4, etc.).
2
3/8 = 3 copies of 1/8 → 1/8+1/8+1/8 = 3/8. ✓
3
The numerator tells you how many unit fractions to add.

B

1

2
Keep the denominator: 3/4. ✓
3
Never add denominators! 1/4+2/4 ≠ 3/8.

Which decimal equals 710?
A
7.0
B
0.7
C
0.07
D
70.0
✓ Answer: B — 0.7
1
Denominator 10 → one decimal place. 7/10 = 0.7. ✓
2
The 7 is in the TENTHS place (first spot after decimal).
3
0.07 = 7/100. 7.0 = 7 wholes. Both wrong.
18
Which fraction equals 0.45?
A
45/10
B
4/5
C
45/100
D
4/10
✓ Answer: C — 45/100
1
0.45 has TWO decimal places → hundredths. 0.45 = 45/100. ✓
2
45/10=4.5 (too big). 4/10=0.4 (different). 4/5=0.80≠0.45.
3
Tip: count decimal places → zeros in denominator. 2 places = /100.
