"The Hidden Garden"

Maya had always wondered what was on the other side of the tall, ivy-covered fence at the end of her street. One Saturday morning, she finally decided to find out. She pushed open the rusty gate and gasped.

Behind it was a garden unlike anything she had ever seen. Wildflowers of every color—purple, yellow, and bright orange—covered the ground in every direction. A stone path wound between the flower beds, and a small fountain in the center bubbled with clear water. Butterflies drifted from blossom to blossom, and the air smelled of honey and fresh earth.

Maya stepped carefully along the path, afraid to disturb the peace of this magical place. She noticed a small wooden sign near the fountain. It read: "Welcome to Mrs. Calloway's Garden. All are welcome to enjoy, but please take only photographs and leave only footprints."

Maya smiled at the kind words. She pulled out her phone and took pictures of every flower she could find. By the time she left, she had made up her mind: she would return every Saturday to help tend this secret paradise.
