🥪 "Leo Makes His Own Lunch" 🥪

One sunny morning, Leo woke up and said, "Today, I will make my own lunch — ALL BY MYSELF!"

His mom raised one eyebrow. His dad tried not to smile. His little sister just stared.

Leo walked to the kitchen. He got out the bread. Plop. He put peanut butter on it. Squiiish. He added jelly. Splat! A big blob of jelly landed right on his nose.

"That is fine," Leo said. "I am still doing this."

He smooshed the two slices together. The sandwich looked a little… lumpy. It had more jelly on one side than the other. One corner had too much peanut butter. But Leo did not give up.

He cut it into two triangles — well, mostly triangles — put it on a plate, and sat down at the table.

He started it. He finished it. ALL BY HIMSELF.

Leo took a big bite. It was the best sandwich he had ever eaten. Maybe because he made it himself. Or maybe because a little jelly was still on his nose.

His dad laughed. His mom clapped. His little sister said, "Can you make MY lunch too?"

Leo smiled and said, "Nope. But I can teach you!"

🥜🍇🥪 THE END 🥪🍇🥜
